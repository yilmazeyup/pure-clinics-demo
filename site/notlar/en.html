What we did well

  1. The Black Friday carousel was November's best post: 17 interactions, against a monthly median of 9. With 1,738 views, that day was the best of the last 28 days.
  2. Interactions more than doubled (+113%). Link clicks +57%.
  3. The right format: carousels and videos get far more views than single photos. Treatment explainers are the most shared topic.
  4. Calm, real visuals: Pure's own photos, little text on the image, the offers in the caption. Everything was scheduled in advance with Neslihan's approval.

Still watching

  1. Story reach: the Black Friday stories were seen by 83–147 people; earlier stories had reached 234–377 people. We will not post the same frames twice within 12 hours.
  2. Evening or morning: on 29 November we will compare the 48-hour result of the 27 November 20:00 post with the 25 November 10:00 post. The posting time will be chosen from the result.
  3. Followers: down 12 net. Only a third of the followers are in the Netherlands, and 99% of interactions come from existing followers. We need to reach new people around Nijmegen, Arnhem and Uden.
  4. Appointments: Meta cannot see the appointments that come in. We will ask Pure how many appointments and gift cards came in over Black Friday.

What we will improve

  1. Fewer single photos: the December kit posts will be carousels (box, contents, price).
  2. Story routine: one series a day, at most 5 frames. The first frame is a question or a price, the last frame a link.
  3. Highlights: "ACTIES" (offers) and "CADEAUBON" (gift card), with cream and walnut covers.
  4. A Christmas giveaway in mid-December. The year's two best posts were giveaways; the January giveaway was viewed 6,648 times. The entry terms will be written out.
  5. Measurement:
    · the website's gift card page is ready;
    · links will get source tags;
    · GA4 and the Meta pixel will be switched on for the website.
  6. Facebook: posts will keep going out automatically. Stories there need no extra effort; only 0–9 people see them.

Part 4

How do Huidly and Pure Clinics work together?

huidly.com is a program made for clinics, and it will replace SkinAdmin. Pure is its first customer. Pure's website, app and admin panel keep Pure's own brand, but they take their data from Huidly and write it back to Huidly. They keep no separate copy of their own.

Pure's website

Treatments, prices, shop, gift card, accounts, booking requests.

Pure app

Skin scan and adviser, appointments, orders, account.
reads and writes

Huidly

One set of data, one source of truth.
  • Diary and booking requests
  • Client records and treatment plans
  • Till, invoices, BTW
  • One stock: clinic and webshop
  • Team, shifts, contracts
  • Treatment and product prices
the team works here

Kliniek

huidly.com/app · Neslihan and the team, sign-in by email code.

Pure's panel

Campaigns, social media plan, webshop. Data from Huidly.

Appointment

The client asks for a day and a time window in the app or on the website. The request lands in Kliniek under "Aanvragen" (requests). The team sets the exact time, and the client sees the confirmation in their own account.

Order

A webshop order comes out of the single stock in Huidly: first from the clinic where it will be collected, otherwise from the other one. The order shows up in Kliniek and in Pure's panel at the same time.

Prices and hours

If a price or an opening time changes in Kliniek, the website and the app show the new one the next time they open. Laser areas and packages are included.

Campaign

Campaign audiences are drawn from the client list and the invoices in Huidly. The social media plan is kept in Huidly too.

Why this way?
  • Nothing is entered twice; the website, the app and the clinic all show the same thing.
  • There is no need for a SkinAdmin subscription any more. Pure's own requests go straight into the program.
  • Pure's brand stays intact: clients do not see Huidly, they see Pure.
  • Huidly can be sold to other clinics and salons too; Pure is the first customer and the example.
  • Where it stands today: it runs live. Pure's real appointments are still in SkinAdmin; moving them over needs an export file from SkinAdmin.
