Pure Clinics · Instagram and Facebook · 27 November 2026

How did Black Friday go, and what should change in December?

In Meta Business Suite we looked at all of Pure's Instagram posts this year, at November's stories, at the followers and at Facebook. Short answers first, then the numbers, and at the end our suggestions for the December plan.

Source: Pure Clinics Meta Business Suite, read on 27 November at 00:10 (Turkish time). Last 28 days = 29 October – 25 November. All times are Dutch time. Story numbers keep rising for 24 hours; the 26 November stories have not finished yet.

Next to the numbers:no mark = read from Metacalculated = our own calculation from the numbers we readestimate = an estimate, not certain

In short

The Black Friday carousel did well.

The post with the four deals on 25 November got the most interactions (likes, comments, shares, saves) of any post in November: 17, against a month median of 9. That day was Instagram's best day of the last 28 days: 1,738 views.

The stories stayed weak.

Each Black Friday story was seen by 83–147 people. The three stories we posted earlier in November had been seen by 234–377 people. We posted the same five frames twice, 12 hours apart; in the second series each frame had fewer viewers than the one before (from 112 down to 83).

The follower count is standing still.

In 28 days 20 people followed and 32 unfollowed: a net loss of 12. Only a third of the followers are in the Netherlands; the rest are in countries where they cannot become Pure's clients.

The best posts of the year are the giveaways.

The January giveaway got 6,648 views, 150 interactions and 33 shares; no other post this year came close. Leaving the giveaways aside, the formats that work best are carousels and videos, and the best topics are treatment explainers and campaigns. Single photos are below average.

Facebook is almost empty.

Posts that went only to Facebook were seen by 66 people on average this year and got 1 interaction on average. On Facebook the Black Friday stories were seen by 0–9 people. The effort should go to Instagram.

Why so low? estimate Three things came together. One: the same frames went up twice within 12 hours, so people watching the second series had already seen them. Two: 5–10 frames came one after another in a single day; Instagram may show an account's stories to fewer people when it posts a lot of them. Three: the frames looked like ads; the earlier stories were more personal.

What should change in December?

The December plan (Sinterklaas, Christmas, New Year) is ready but has not gone to Neslihan yet. Based on the numbers, we suggest these changes to the plan.

  1. 30 November · gift card

    A carousel or a short video instead of a single photo.

    Instead of the single photo in the plan (05-cadeaubon), 3 slides: the card, "Buy a €100 card, get €15 extra", and where to buy it. Or the ready-made 10-second gift card video. On the same day, the same slides once as stories, with a link. Note: the site has no gift card page yet (Neslihan asked for one too). Until the page is there, the story link can go to the booking page.

  2. 2 December · Christmas carousel

    The format is right; post it at 10:00.

    In the plan every post goes out at 19:30. We are testing the evening for the first time with the 27 November 20:00 laser post. On 29 November let's compare that post's 48-hour numbers with the 25 November 10:00 post. If the evening does better, we switch to evenings from 4 December; if not, we stay with mornings. Until then, 10:00.

  3. 7 and 11 December · kit posts

    Let's use fewer single photos.

    The December plan has 7 single photos. Let's turn the kit posts into carousels: the box, what's inside, the price. The greetings posts (23 and 29 December) can stay single photos.

  4. Every day · stories

    One series a day, at most 5 frames, each frame only once.

    As Neslihan wanted, the post's slides go out as stories on the day of the post. But let's not post the same frames a second time within 24 hours. The first frame is a clear question or a price, the last frame is the link. In between, a question card such as "Which kit suits you?", for replies by message.

  5. Highlights

    Keep the deals on the profile.

    Let's put the Black Friday frames in the "ACTIES" (offers) highlight until 1 December, then replace them with the Christmas kits. A separate "CADEAUBON" (gift card) highlight for the gift card: how to buy it, how long it is valid. Highlight covers in cream and walnut. If Neslihan approves.

  6. Mid-December

    A Christmas giveaway.

    The year's two best posts were giveaways. For a ZO kit or a gift card: follow us, tag a friend. It is the quickest way to bring in new local followers. The entry rules must be written down (in the Netherlands, giveaway rules have to be published). To be suggested to Neslihan.

  7. Facebook

    Posts go over automatically; stories can be skipped.

    Feed posts should keep being shared to Facebook automatically. Facebook stories reach 0–9 people, so they are not worth extra effort when planning.

  8. Measuring

    Count appointments, not likes.

    Meta cannot see how many appointments came from Black Friday. Let's ask Neslihan how many appointments and gift cards came in during BF. Let's add source tags to the links so that visitors from Instagram are counted separately.

Also: Pure's inbox has messages from unknown accounts saying "your page has earned a free Meta Verified badge". These are fake: do not click them and do not enter a password. It is worth telling Neslihan and the team.
