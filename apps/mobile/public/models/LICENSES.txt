Pure Clinics huidscan models

huidscan.onnx
  MobileNetV3-large (timm, Apache-2.0), trained by Pure Clinics on its own before/after photos and on public data.
  Trained in part on the SCIN dataset (Google Research), SCIN Data Use License,
  https://github.com/google-research-datasets/scin

huidscan-pigment-evren.onnx, huidscan-roodheid-evren.onnx
  Ultralytics YOLO26s-cls classifiers, trained on EVREN (evren.ssyz.org.tr) on public data only:
  SCIN (SCIN Data Use License) and PAD-UFES-20 (Pacheco et al., 2020, CC BY 4.0,
  https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/zr7vgbcyr2/1). Weights stored as int8 (first and last layer fp32).
  Licence: AGPL-3.0 (https://ultralytics.com/license, https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html).
  Source of the app that runs them, the scripts that made the training data and the int8 copies:
  https://github.com/yilmazeyup/pure-clinics-demo (apps/mobile/src/scan/model.ts, ml/huidscan/).

The models describe what is visible on the skin. They do not give a medical diagnosis.
